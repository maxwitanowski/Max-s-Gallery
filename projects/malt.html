Compare

One prompt, every model, answers in parallel. The fastest way to find out which model is actually good at the thing you are asking, instead of guessing.

  1. Switch to the Compare tab in the header.
  2. Add the models you want from the sidebar with "Add model".
  3. Send one prompt. They all answer at once, side by side.
  4. Drag, resize or dismiss cards, then hit Analysis for a synthesis of all of them.

Round Table

The models talk to each other, not just to you. They take turns, read what the others said, change their minds, and keep going until they agree. Then a moderator writes the verdict.

  1. In the Swarm tab, choose "Round Table".
  2. Add the models you want seated at the table.
  3. Send a prompt. They discuss in turns, reading each other's points.
  4. They continue until consensus, then a moderator writes the final answer. Pause any time to step in yourself.

Handoff

An assembly line. One model drafts, the next edits, the last one polishes. Useful when one model writes well and another is better at cutting.

  1. Open the Swarm tab and choose "Handoff".
  2. Add two or more models. They appear as cards on the canvas.
  3. Tap one model's right dot, then another's left dot, to set the order.
  4. Send a prompt. Each model refines the previous draft down the chain.

Live Canvas

When the answer is a chart, a page or a small app, Malt runs it instead of just printing the code at you.

  1. Ask for a chart, web page or small app in normal chat.
  2. Charts render inline. Full pages and apps open in the side Canvas.
  3. Everything runs in a safe sandbox, with no setup needed.
  4. Open any code block in the Canvas with the Canvas button.

Getting started

Sign in, type, send. Swap the model mid-conversation whenever you feel like it.

  1. Create an account or sign in. You get a starter token balance automatically.
  2. Type a message at the bottom and press Enter.
  3. Pick which model answers from the selector above the input.
  4. Tap the microphone to dictate instead, or toggle Deep Thinking and Search.

How the site works

Each card opens the matching part of the preview.

Market

One chart, eight stocks, live.

Prices come from Yahoo's chart API every minute for 24 ASX 200 large caps and 24 US mega caps. Up to eight stocks overlay on a single normalised chart across 1H to 1Y ranges, with a trading-time axis so closed hours collapse instead of drawing long flat lines.

Find opportunities

An AI scan of the whole board.

The scan researches the macro picture first (RBA and Fed, commodities, China, AI capex) and then sorts picks into bangers, quality dips, momentum and small-cap catalysts, each with a why, a what-to-watch and a risk line. Every ticker is validated against Yahoo before it's shown.

Trading

Fake money against real prices.

A$1,000 to start. Fills use the live price plus a simulated spread and my real broker's fee schedule, with stops, targets, an ASX opening-auction queue, shorting and a 2× leverage cap. The portfolio tab keeps the equity curve, allocation and every trade.

You vs AI

The investment war.

Both books reset and the machine switches on. The scoreboard tracks me, the AI and what the same money would have done in the S&P 500 or ASX 200, with equity curves for all four. Every AI trade is logged with the reasoning behind it.

The committee

Five analysts and a chair.

A macro strategist, momentum scout, hedge-fund tracker, fundamentals analyst and risk officer each research fresh with web search, debate, converge on a joint portfolio, and a portfolio manager sets the targets. The engine executes them. They follow the sun: both opens, both closes, the China window.

Memory and rules

It remembers its mistakes.

Every agent keeps a permanent memory of what it got wrong and why, and a post-mortem runs after any sell. Standing rules (no re-entry within three hours, minimum hold, three trades a session), a daily loss cap, a drawdown kill switch and a red-day mode keep it honest. You can talk to any member from the chat drawer.

Ledger

Gates before real money.

Round trips, expectancy per playbook, weekly spendable dollars and benchmarks against buy-and-hold. The AI only graduates to a real broker if it beats me and the index for four to eight weeks with a drawdown under 15%. It hasn't yet.

Settings

Keys, models, brokers.

OpenRouter key and model per role, committee size and schedule, broker fee preset, leverage cap, watchlist. The key never leaves my machine: it sits in a local data folder that Docker mounts.

Under the hood

The real Night Desk runs in Docker on my PC and keeps trading while the browser is closed. The preview above is the same interface with generated prices and a scripted committee, so there's nothing to break and no API key involved.

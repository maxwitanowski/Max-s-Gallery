    How stuff gets hereSame three steps every time.

    Get obsessed, build it, hang it up.

    There's no plan. Something looks fun, I make it, and once it works it gets its own look and a spot on the wall.

    1. 01 — Get obsessed

      An idea shows up and won't leave.

      Usually at 11pm. Usually something I've never built before, which is the whole point.

    2. 02 — Build it for real

      Working code, not mockups.

      If it's a visualiser, it runs. If it's a game, you can play it. Screenshots are boring.

    3. 03 — Give it a look

      Every project gets its own outfit.

      Brutalist, pastel, paper, neon. Then it goes on the wall and the glass shuffles up to make room.
